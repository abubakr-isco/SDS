End users opt in directly through the SDS UZ INC website at https://sdsuz-inc.com/.

Customers complete the Request a Freight Quote form and enter their mobile phone number. An optional, unchecked-by-default SMS consent checkbox is displayed with the complete SMS disclosure.

Customers must manually check the box to consent to receive SMS messages from SDS UZ INC regarding freight quote follow-ups, shipment updates, delivery notifications, and customer support.

The consent checkbox is optional. Customers can submit a quote request without consenting to receive SMS messages. Providing a phone number alone does not constitute consent.

The website records consent status, timestamp, disclosure version, and form source.

Privacy Policy: https://sdsuz-inc.com/privacy

SMS Terms & Conditions: https://sdsuz-inc.com/sms-terms

No SMS messages are sent to customers who have not explicitly opted in.

How each statement was checked (2026-11-04, live site https://sdsuz-inc.com)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Opt-in on the website quote form: the form at https://sdsuz-inc.com/#quote ("Request a
  Freight Quote", reached from the "Get a freight quote" button) has a "Phone number" field
  followed directly by a separate "Text message updates - Optional" checkbox with the
  disclosure above. Screenshots 01, 02, 03.
- Unchecked by default, not required: verified in the browser on the live site; the
  checkbox has no "required" attribute.
- A quote can be sent without consent: a submission with the box unchecked reaches the
  confirmation screen (screenshot 06) and is recorded as sms_consent = false.
- Phone number alone is not consent: consent is true only when the box is checked.
- What is recorded: every request emailed to dispatch@sdsuzinc.com contains sms_consent
  (true/false), consent_phone, consent_timestamp (UTC, only when consent was given),
  consent_source (website_quote_form), consent_page (form URL), consent_version,
  consent_text (exact wording shown) and submission_id. The subject line also says
  "SMS consent: yes" or "SMS consent: no".
- Links: both links are clickable and load (HTTP 200).


Before pasting, confirm that these statements are true for how SDS UZ INC works
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. "No SMS messages are sent to customers who have not explicitly opted in" is a promise
   about how your team sends texts from RingCentral. The website can't enforce it. Text
   only numbers whose quote email says "SMS consent: yes".
2. If you text customers who agreed some other way (by email, by phone, in person), the
   statement "End users opt in directly through the website" is not true for them. Ask
   those customers to opt in on the website form, or don't text them.
3. STOP and HELP are handled by RingCentral, not by this website. Once the campaign is
   active, send STOP and HELP from a personal phone to the SDS number and check the replies.
