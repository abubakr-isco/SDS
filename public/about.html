About SDS UZ INC

SDS UZ INC is a trucking and freight transportation company. We move freight for businesses that need it delivered safely, efficiently and on time.

What we do

At SDS UZ INC, we believe transportation is about more than moving freight. It’s about helping businesses stay connected, meet their commitments, and keep moving forward. Our goal is to build dependable relationships through professional service, clear communication, and a practical approach to transportation.

Our services

  • Full Truckload (FTL) Dedicated truck capacity for larger shipments and business-critical freight.
  • Less Than Truckload (LTL) Flexible shipping options for freight that does not require a full trailer.
  • Long-Haul Transportation Transportation solutions for shipments moving across longer distances.
  • Regional Freight Transportation support for regional routes and recurring shipping needs.
  • Dedicated Transportation Consistent transportation capacity for businesses with ongoing requirements.
  • Logistics Coordination Practical coordination to help manage freight movements and delivery expectations.

Our equipment

Sleeper cab tractors, dry van trailers, refrigerated trailers and flatbed trailers.

How we communicate with customers

Customers reach us by phone, email or through our quote request form. Customers who choose to opt in on that form can also receive text messages from SDS UZ INC about quote follow-ups, shipment and delivery updates, and customer support replies. See our SMS Terms & Conditions.
