Privacy Policy

SDS UZ INC · Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Who we are

SDS UZ INC ("we", "us") operates sdsuz-inc.onrender.com. Contact: dispatch@sdsuzinc.com, (215) 268-3886.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, phone number, email address.
  • Information about your orders or requests: freight details such as pickup and delivery locations, dates, equipment type and cargo description.
  • Basic website usage data, such as browser type and pages visited.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide and manage our services and respond to your requests.
  • To send you text messages you have agreed to receive, such as quote follow-ups, shipment and delivery updates, and customer support replies.
  • To keep records and comply with legal obligations.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate our business (for example hosting and messaging delivery providers), and only as needed to provide our services, or when required by law.

5. SMS / mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS consent and opt-in data are not shared with third parties or affiliates. Information shared with messaging providers is used only to deliver our messages.

6. Your choices

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information by emailing dispatch@sdsuzinc.com.

7. Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new date at the top of this page.
