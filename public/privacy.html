Privacy Policy

SDS UZ INC · Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Who we are

SDS UZ INC ("we", "us") operates sdsuz-inc.com. Contact: dispatch@sdsuzinc.com, (215) 268-3886, 133 Carters Mill RD, Feasterville Trevose, PA 19053.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, phone number, email address.
  • Information about your orders or requests: freight details such as pickup and delivery locations, dates, equipment type and cargo description.
  • Basic website usage data, such as browser type and pages visited.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide and manage our services and respond to your requests.
  • To send you text messages you have agreed to receive, such as quote follow-ups, shipment and delivery updates, and customer support replies.
  • To keep records and comply with legal obligations.

4. Whether and with whom we share personal information

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. We share personal information only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate our business: our website hosting provider, the service that delivers our website form submissions to us by email, and our text messaging provider. They may use the information only to provide those services to us.
  • Government authorities or other parties, when required by law.

All of the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

5. SMS / mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates.

We text you only if you opt in using the SMS consent box on our quote request form. Browsing this website, or giving us a phone number without checking that box, does not sign you up for text messages. See our SMS Terms & Conditions.

6. Your choices

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, and reply HELP for help. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information by emailing dispatch@sdsuzinc.com.

7. Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new date at the top of this page.
