Program description

By giving us your mobile number and agreeing to receive texts, you consent to receive messages from SDS UZ INC, such as quote follow-ups, shipment and delivery updates, and customer support replies.

Consent

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase. You can give consent by checking the SMS consent box on our quote request form at sdsuz-inc.com/#quote. The box is optional and unchecked by default, and it reads:

I agree to receive text messages from SDS UZ INC about quote follow-ups, shipment and delivery updates, and customer support replies at the mobile number provided. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Browsing this website, or entering a phone number on the form without checking the box, does not sign you up for text messages.

Message frequency

Messaging frequency may vary.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out

To opt out at any time, text STOP. You will receive one confirmation message and no further texts unless you opt in again.

Help

For help, text HELP or visit our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions. You can also email dispatch@sdsuzinc.com or call (215) 268-3886.

Privacy
