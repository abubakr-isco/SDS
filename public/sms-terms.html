SMS Terms & Conditions

SDS UZ INC · Last updated: November 2, 2026

Program name

SDS UZ INC SMS Notifications, operated by SDS UZ INC.

Program description

SDS UZ INC SMS Notifications sends text messages only to customers who opt in on our website. Messages are about freight you have asked us about or shipped with us:

  • Freight quote follow-ups, such as questions about a quote request or the quote itself.
  • Shipment updates, such as pickup confirmations and changes to a schedule.
  • Delivery notifications, such as arrival and delivery confirmations.
  • Customer support replies to questions you send us.

We do not send marketing or promotional text messages.

How to opt in

You can opt in by checking the SMS consent box on our quote request form at https://sdsuz-inc.com/#quote, next to the phone number field. The box is optional and unchecked by default. You can send a quote request without checking it. It reads:

By checking this box, I agree to receive SMS messages from SDS UZ INC regarding freight quote follow-ups, shipment updates, delivery notifications, and customer support.

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for assistance.

Consent is not a condition of purchase.

See our Privacy Policy and SMS Terms & Conditions.

Browsing this website, calling or emailing us, or entering a phone number on the form without checking the box does not sign you up for text messages. When you opt in, we record your phone number, the date and time (UTC), the form you used, the exact wording you agreed to, and your request ID.

Consent is not a condition of purchase

You do not have to agree to receive text messages to request a quote or to buy any service from SDS UZ INC.

Message frequency

Message frequency may vary, depending on your quote requests and shipments.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. Check your mobile plan for details.

Opt out

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe at any time. You will receive one confirmation message and no further texts unless you opt in again. You can also ask us to stop texting you by email or phone, using the details below.

Help

Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or visit our website at https://sdsuz-inc.com.
